# OKS Writ Shopper

A WoW Forever addon for Craftsman's Writs.

- `/writs` (or `/ws`) opens the writ list. Filter by the delivery NPC's zones (tick several) and profession,
  tick "Only writs I have" to see writs in your bags or quest log, and sort by zone and
  position (NPCs standing close together end up next to each other) or by distance from you.
- Click a row to tick it. Right-click a row for a waypoint to the NPC (TomTom too if installed) and the world map at its zone.
- "Show on map": world map pins for the NPCs of the ticked writs (or all shown), with their spot letter and colour.
- "Add to buy list" adds each ticked writ (unless its quest is already in your log) and the item it asks for, minus what you already own.
- At the auction house the Buy List opens beside it: Check prices, then one click per step.
  Commodities: click to get the quote, click to confirm. Gear: one click per auction.
- `/writs buy` toggles the buy list, `/writs clear` empties it.

## Data

`Data.lua` is generated by `tools/build_data.py` from addons installed locally:
WaylaidHelper (MIT, writ list), WaylaidCost (NPC names and coordinates) and NaowhForever
(professions, zone map IDs). Re-run it after those addons update:

    python tools/build_data.py "C:/Program Files (x86)/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns" Data.lua

WaylaidCost ships no licence, so ask its author before publishing this addon with that data.

## Crates & Receipts (`/writs rep`, or the "Crates & Receipts" tab)

- Waylaid Crates ranked by cost per Merchant's Favor: crate price + cheapest bundle - money the
  Sealed Crate pays back, divided by the favor it pays (5/10/15/20 white, 10/20/30/40 green).
- "Favor to earn": the cheapest mix of crates for that much favor (knapsack), added with its bundles.
- "Reputation wanted": compares writs (75/125/200 rep, once a day each) with Forged Delivery
  Receipts (15 favor + an empty crate for 50 rep), takes writs while they are cheaper per rep,
  covers the rest with receipts, and adds everything needed to the buy list.
- Crate data and the favor/rep values come from WaylaidHelper (MIT); receipt values from WaylaidCost.
